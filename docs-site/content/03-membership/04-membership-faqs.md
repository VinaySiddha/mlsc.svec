# Membership FAQs

---

### Q: Is membership valid for my entire academic tenure?
**A:** Yes. Once registered, your general member account remains active throughout your tenure at Sri Vasavi Engineering College. Core volunteer and lead roles are reviewed and renewed on an annual basis.

---

### Q: Are there any membership fees?
**A:** No. General registration and access to the Study Hub, Discord community, and standard peer-learning sessions are 100% free. Certain specialized physical hackathons or external certified bootcamps may carry nominal material/venue fees which are always transparently published.

---

### Q: Can non-CSE students join and contribute to technical projects?
**A:** Absolutely. Some of our most impactful developers and leads come from ECE, EEE, and Mechanical departments. Passion and willingness to build matter far more than branch labels.

---

### Q: What happens if I violate the Code of Conduct?
**A:** Depending on the severity of the violation, actions range from a formal warning to removal from official channels and permanent revocation of club certificates.
