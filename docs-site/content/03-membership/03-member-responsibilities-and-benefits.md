# Member Responsibilities & Benefits

---

## 1. Member Responsibilities

To maintain an active and respectful learning ecosystem, members are expected to:
- **Uphold Community Guidelines:** Follow the MLSC Code of Conduct across all online channels and physical events.
- **Maintain Academic & Ethical Integrity:** Do not engage in plagiarism, cheating in coding contests, or abusing shared compute resources.
- **Support Peer Learning:** Actively help fellow students in `#dsa-help` and `#dev-talk` channels.
- **Respect Intellectual Property:** Attribute original creators when sharing open-source code and assets.
