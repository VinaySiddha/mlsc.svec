# Communication Guidelines

Official communication from MLSC SVEC must reflect our core identity: modern, student-centric, encouraging, and technically rigorous.

---

## 1. Brand Tone & Voice

- **Encouraging & Accessible:** Avoid elitist jargon. Welcome questions from complete beginners.
- **Concise & Action-Oriented:** Get straight to the point. State event time, location, prerequisites, and registration links immediately.
- **Professional & Transparent:** When discussing scheduling changes or technical outages, communicate proactively and honestly.

---

## 2. Formatting & Channel Standards

- Use clear markdown structure (bullet points, bold key phrases, clear headings).
- Always include full, valid URLs starting with `https://`.
- Never use `@everyone` or `@here` on Discord without prior authorization from the Operations Lead.
