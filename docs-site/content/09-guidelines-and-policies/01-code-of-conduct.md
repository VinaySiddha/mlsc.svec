# Code of Conduct

MLSC SVEC is dedicated to providing a safe, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, or technical skill level.

---

## 1. Expected Behaviors

- **Be Respectful & Empathetic:** Encourage beginner questions. Treat every community member with patience and dignity.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** When reviewing code or designs, provide actionable suggestions rather than disparaging feedback.
- **Academic & Professional Honesty:** Respect intellectual property; give attribution to original creators; never cheat in competitions.

---

## 2. Unacceptable Behaviors

- Offensive verbal comments or jokes related to gender, disability, physical appearance, or race.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments in Discord, WhatsApp, or GitHub pull requests.
- Sustained disruption of talks, workshops, or virtual meeting streams.
- Unauthorized publishing of private information (doxxing).

---

## 3. Enforcement & Reporting

Violations should be reported directly to `conduct@mlscsvec.com` or via direct contact with the Lead Ambassador or Faculty Advisor. Violators may be warned, temporarily muted, or permanently expelled from the organization.
