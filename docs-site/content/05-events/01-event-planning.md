# Event Planning & Lifecycle

Every event conducted by MLSC SVEC follows a standardized 4-week preparation pipeline to ensure high educational quality and smooth execution.

---

## 1. The 4-Week Event Timeline

```text
[T-4 Weeks] ────────> [T-3 Weeks] ────────> [T-2 Weeks] ────────> [T-1 Week] ────────> [Event Day]
Concept Formulation    Faculty Approval     Portal Setup &       Intensive Promotion    Live On-Site
& Speaker Outreach     & Lab Allocation     Poster Release       & Capacity Lock        Execution
```

- **T-4 Weeks (Ideation & Proposal):** Domain Leads define learning outcomes, draft session curriculum, and contact prospective speakers or student mentors.
- **T-3 Weeks (Institutional Approval):** Submit the Event Proposal Document to the Faculty Advisor and HOD CSE for venue booking.
- **T-2 Weeks (Platform Setup & Marketing Kickoff):** Publish the event on `mlscsvec.com/events`, release teaser posters, and open registration.
- **T-1 Week (Capacity Verification):** Review ticket sales/registrations, send confirmation emails with calendar invites, and finalize physical lab arrangements.
- **Event Day (Live Ops):** Check-in scanning, speaker facilitation, live coding sessions, and Q&A.

---

## 2. Standard Event Proposal Template

When proposing an event, the organizing lead must draft a document containing:
1. **Event Name & Subtitle**
2. **Target Audience:** (e.g. 1st & 2nd Year B.Tech, All Branches)
3. **Proposed Date, Time, and Venue:** (e.g. CSE Central Lab 3)
4. **Learning Objectives & Deliverables:** What will students build?
5. **Detailed Agenda Breakdown:** Minute-by-minute session schedule.
6. **Hardware & Software Prerequisites:** Required tools (Node.js, VS Code, Azure CLI, Python 3.11).
7. **Budget Breakdown:** (Mementoes, refreshments, printed badges).
8. **Organizing Team Leads & Point of Contact.**
