# Event FAQs

---

### Q: Where can I find my event ticket / pass?
**A:** Once registered, your pass with unique QR token is sent to your registered email address and is also accessible on your profile at `https://mlscsvec.com/profile`.

---

### Q: Are spot registrations allowed on the day of the event?
**A:** Spot registrations are subject to lab seat availability and are managed through the on-site registration desk. To guarantee seating and workshop kits, online registration via `mlscsvec.com/events` is strongly encouraged.

---

### Q: When will I receive my event certificate?
**A:** Certificates are issued automatically within 48 to 72 hours following attendance verification. You will receive an email with direct download links, and the certificate will appear under your verified profile.

---

### Q: What should I do if my payment succeeded but the registration failed?
**A:** Raise a ticket immediately via the floating bug/ticket widget on `mlscsvec.com` or email support at `support@mlscsvec.com` with your Razorpay payment transaction ID. Our payment admin will reconcile and verify your ticket within 2 hours.
