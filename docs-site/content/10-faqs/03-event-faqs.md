# Event & Ticketing FAQs

---

### Q: Do I need to bring my own laptop to hands-on workshops?
**A:** If the event is scheduled in college computer labs, desktops with required environments are provided. For hackathons and bring-your-own-device (BYOD) bootcamps, personal laptops with power adapters are required.

---

### Q: Can external participants from other colleges attend MLSC SVEC events?
**A:** Yes, for inter-college hackathons and state-level technical symposiums, external students are warmly welcomed. Specific registration instructions will be noted on the event page.

---

### Q: What should I do if I lose my registration confirmation email?
**A:** Simply log in to [https://mlscsvec.com/profile](https://mlscsvec.com/profile). All your active event registrations and QR check-in passes are permanently stored there.
