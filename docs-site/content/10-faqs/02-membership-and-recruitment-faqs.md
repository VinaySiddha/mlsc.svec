# Membership & Recruitment FAQs

---

### Q: What qualities does the recruitment panel look for?
**A:** We look for curiosity, consistency, willingness to learn, and team spirit. While existing technical or design skills are valuable, enthusiasm and dependability are paramount.

---

### Q: Can 1st-year students apply for Core Volunteer roles?
**A:** Yes! We actively encourage passionate 1st-year students to apply. Many 1st-year volunteers grow into Domain Leads and Ambassadors in subsequent years.

---

### Q: If I am not selected as a volunteer, can I still participate in club activities?
**A:** Absolutely. General membership is open to everyone. You can use the Study Hub, participate in all bootcamps and hackathons, and contribute to open-source repositories via GitHub.
