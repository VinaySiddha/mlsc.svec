# General FAQs

---

### Q: What is the relationship between Microsoft and MLSC SVEC?
**A:** MLSC SVEC is a student-led club chartered under the global Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors (MLSA) program. We receive access to curriculum resources, student cloud credits, and event support from Microsoft, while operating autonomously under the CSE Department at Sri Vasavi Engineering College.

---

### Q: How can I contact the club leadership?
**A:** Reach out via:
- **Email:** `contact@mlscsvec.com`
- **Discord:** `#ask-leads` channel
- **Contact Page:** [https://mlscsvec.com/contact](https://mlscsvec.com/contact)

---

### Q: Where is the MLSC SVEC office / hub located?
**A:** Our primary physical operations are based in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Sri Vasavi Engineering College campus, Pedatadepalli, Tadepalligudem.
