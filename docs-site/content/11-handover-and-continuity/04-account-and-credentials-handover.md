# Account & Credentials Handover

Transferring club accounts requires strict adherence to security protocols to avoid lockouts and protect access credentials.

---

## 1. Managed Account Inventory

- **Primary Google Workspace / Club Email:** `contact@mlscsvec.com`, `admin@mlscsvec.com`
- **Social Media Handles:** LinkedIn Page, Instagram (`@mlsc_svec`), X/Twitter, YouTube Channel
- **Design Workspaces:** Figma Organization & Canva Pro
- **Communication Hubs:** Discord Server (Ownership Transfer), WhatsApp Community, Telegram Channels

---

## 2. Secure Transition Steps

1. **Vault Master Transfer:** Grant primary ownership of the encrypted password manager to the incoming President and Tech Lead.
2. **Rotate 2FA Phone Numbers:** Update 2FA mobile numbers on all accounts from outgoing seniors to incoming leads.
3. **Backup Recovery Codes:** Regenerate and safely store new recovery codes in the physical and digital vaults.
4. **Remove Former Devices:** Invalidate active sessions and trusted browser tokens from graduating members' personal laptops.
