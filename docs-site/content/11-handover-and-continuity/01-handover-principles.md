# Handover Principles & Continuity Doctrine

Student organizations naturally face annual leadership turnover as senior members graduate. Without structured handover procedures, critical knowledge, account accesses, and project context are lost.

---

## 1. The Zero-Knowledge-Loss Doctrine

> **"A newly appointed leadership team should be fully capable of deploying applications, managing events, moderating communities, and resolving technical incidents on Day 1 without relying on the physical presence of graduating seniors."**

To enforce this doctrine:
1. Every piece of infrastructure must be documented on `docs.mlscsvec.com`.
2. All production credentials must be centralized in the club password manager.
3. No critical service may be tied solely to a student's personal phone number or personal email address.

---

## 2. The Annual Transition Timeline

```text
[APRIL] ────────────────> [MAY] ─────────────────> [JUNE] ────────────────> [JULY]
Successor Nominations     Shadowing &             Credentials & Repo        New Leadership
& Council Review          Co-Management           Owner Transfers           Full Autonomy
```

- **April:** Identify, interview, and confirm incoming Domain Leads and Ambassadors.
- **May (Shadowing):** Incoming leads shadow outgoing leads across meetings, deployments, and event ops.
- **June (Formal Handover):** Formal transfer of GitHub Organization Ownership, Cloudflare admin rights, Firebase roles, and password vaults.
- **July:** Incoming team assumes complete independent governance for the new academic year.
