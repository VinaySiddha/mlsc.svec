# Admin & Leadership Handover Protocol

---

## 1. Executive Council Transition Protocol

Outgoing Presidents and Vice Presidents must conduct formal transition briefings covering:
1. **Institutional Relationships:** Introduce incoming leaders to the Faculty Advisor, HOD CSE, and College IT Administrators.
2. **Microsoft Learn Program Status:** Transfer MLSA portal management, program manager contact lines, and community swag/voucher request workflows.
3. **Financial Ledger & Balances:** Reconcile all outstanding bank/cash balances and transfer treasury ledger controls.
4. **Active Contracts & Sponsorships:** Document ongoing sponsor relationships, brand agreements, and speaker alumni networks.

---

## 2. Portal Administration Handover

1. Promote incoming lead accounts to `admin` in `mlscsvec.com/admin/team`.
2. Ensure incoming leads have verified access to `/admin/events`, `/admin/payments`, and `/admin/bulk-mail`.
3. Revoke outgoing student admin privileges once final graduation clearance is reached.
