# Admin Responsibilities & Ethical Code

Portal and system administrators are the custodians of MLSC SVEC's digital infrastructure, member data, and community trust.

---

## 1. Core Administrative Obligations

Admins must:
- **Legitimate Use Only:** Use elevated access strictly for authorized, official club operations. Personal curiosity, data scraping, or unauthorized modifications are grounds for immediate expulsion.
- **Maintain Absolute Impartiality:** Evaluate volunteer applicants, hackathon submissions, and community disputes purely on documented merit and evidence, without bias or favoritism.
- **Protect Confidentiality:** Safeguard student phone numbers, email addresses, payment records, and interview notes from unauthorized third-party disclosure.
- **Ensure Operational Accuracy:** Double-check all live database mutations, bulk email dispatches, and public announcements before execution.
- **Maintain Living Documentation:** Log every infrastructure modification, environment variable change, or DNS update directly on `docs.mlscsvec.com`.
