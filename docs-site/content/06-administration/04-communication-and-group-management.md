# Communication & Community Group Management

MLSC SVEC maintains active community hubs across WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, and LinkedIn. Proper moderation preserves a safe, focused, and professional learning environment.

---

## 1. Moderation & Channel Rules

- **Zero Tolerance for Spam:** Commercial advertisements, unauthorized referral links, chain forwards, and unsolicited self-promotions will result in immediate message deletion and user banning.
- **Respectful Discourse:** Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, or political arguments are strictly prohibited.
- **Channel Specificity:** Encourage members to post queries in the appropriate Discord channel (e.g., `#dsa-help`, `#web-dev`, `#cloud-ops`).

---

## 2. Official Announcement Guidelines

To prevent notification fatigue:
1. **Frequency:** Limit broad announcements to a maximum of 2 high-priority broadcasts per week, except during live multi-day hackathons.
2. **Standard Format:**
   - **Header:** [EMOJI] **[EVENT / ANNOUNCEMENT TITLE]**
   - **Summary:** 2-sentence crisp description of what is happening.
   - **Details:** Date, Time, Venue, and Target Audience.
   - **Call to Action:** Direct clickable link (`https://mlscsvec.com/...`).
3. **Admin Exclusivity:** Announcement groups on WhatsApp and Telegram must remain configured with "Only Admins Can Send Messages."
