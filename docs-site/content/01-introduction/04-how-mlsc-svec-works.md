# How MLSC SVEC Works

MLSC SVEC functions through structured operating cycles, transparent digital workflows, and proactive communication.

---

## 1. The Annual Operating Cycle

```text
[JUL - AUG] ──────> [SEP - OCT] ──────> [NOV - JAN] ──────> [FEB - APR] ──────> [MAY - JUN]
Core Council        Recruitment         Flagship           Annual              Handover &
Planning &          Drive &             Bootcamps &        Hackathon &         Knowledge
Syllabus Design     Onboarding          Study Sprints      Tech Fest           Archiving
```

1. **July – August (Strategic Planning):**
   - Review platform systems, update `docs.mlscsvec.com`, prepare domain curricula, and define the semester calendar.
2. **September – October (Recruitment & Onboarding):**
   - Launch public recruitment portal (`/apply`), conduct evaluation rounds, and onboard new core volunteers.
3. **November – January (Hands-on Bootcamps & Study Sprints):**
   - Intensive technical series (Full-Stack development, Cloud Fundamentals, GenAI pipelines, DSA Leaderboard Sprints).
4. **February – April (Flagship Hackathons & Competitions):**
   - Large-scale multi-day hackathons, competitive programming contests, and project exhibitions.
5. **May – June (Leadership Succession & Documentation Handover):**
   - Rigorous audit of infrastructure, credential handover, and publishing updated institutional documentation.

---

## 2. Weekly & Monthly Rhythms

- **Weekly Syncs (Sundays, 8:00 PM):**
  - 30-minute high-efficiency virtual standup across Domain Leads. Reviewing active blockers, feature PRs, and upcoming event milestones.
- **Bi-Weekly Code Review & Deployments:**
  - Merging vetted feature branches into `Dev` and staging deployments for `mlscsvec.com`.
- **Monthly Community Townhalls:**
  - Live technical sessions, leaderboard recognitions, and project demonstrations by student builders.

---

## 3. Communication & Operating Channels

| Purpose | Official Channel | Access Policy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Official Announcements** | WhatsApp Official Community & Telegram Broadcast | Read-only broadcast by Admins |
| **Technical Discussions & Help** | Discord Server (`#general`, `#dsa-help`, `#dev-talk`) | Public to all verified members |
| **Internal Team Collaboration** | Discord Private Category & Internal Workspaces | Role-restricted to Leads & Volunteers |
| **Code & Infrastructure** | GitHub (`github.com/MLSC-SVEC`) | Public repositories + Private core teams |
| **Single Source of Truth Docs** | [https://docs.mlscsvec.com](https://docs.mlscsvec.com) | Publicly accessible to the world |
