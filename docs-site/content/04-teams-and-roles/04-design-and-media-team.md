# Design & Media Team

The Design & Media Division shapes the visual identity, brand aesthetics, and user experience across digital portals and physical events.

---

## 1. Core Responsibilities

- **UI/UX Design:** Wireframing, prototyping, and user journey mapping in Figma for `mlscsvec.com` and all internal software tools.
- **Brand Identity & Graphics:** Creating official posters, banner artwork, social media graphics, badges, ID cards, and speaker cards.
- **Video & Motion Graphics:** Producing high-energy promotional teasers, workshop recap reels, event after-movies, and animated motion assets.
- **Stage & Venue Visuals:** Designing physical standees, projector backdrops, certificates, and presentation slide templates.

---

## 2. Design Standards & Asset Workflow

1. **Figma Centralization:** All official design files must reside in the shared MLSC SVEC Figma Organization space.
2. **Color Palette Adherence:** Use the official Microsoft brand palette (`#4285F4`, `#34A853`, `#FBBC05`, `#EA4335`, and dark surface colors `#0A0A0A`, `#000000`).
3. **Resolution & Formats:** Export posters at high resolution (300 DPI for print, 1080x1350 for Instagram portrait, 1200x630 for OpenGraph).
4. **Approval Gate:** Final artwork must be reviewed by the Design Lead and President before publication.
