# Event Management Team

The Event Management Division is responsible for the end-to-end physical and virtual execution of workshops, bootcamps, speaker sessions, and hackathons.

---

## 1. Responsibilities Breakdown

- **Venue & Infrastructure Prep:** Coordinating lab allocations, testing internet bandwidth, projector systems, wireless mics, and power extension arrays.
- **On-Site Ticketing & Fast Check-In:** Operating the digital QR scanning desks at event entrances to check in attendees within seconds.
- **Stage & Host Coordination:** Managing the master of ceremonies (emcee), speaker hospitality, timekeeping, and audience Q&A mics.
- **Discipline & Crowd Flow:** Ensuring orderly seating, lab hardware protection, and emergency egress routing.
- **Post-Event Teardown:** Restoring computer labs to pristine state, securing club equipment, and compiling physical attendance sheets.

---

## 2. On-Site Operations Checklist

- **T-2 Hours:** Complete audio/video and projector test run; verify high-speed network connectivity.
- **T-1 Hour:** Open registration desks; test QR code scanner scanners with live Firestore database sync.
- **T-0 (Event Start):** Signal emcee; facilitate speaker entry; manage overflow seating.
- **Post-Event:** Hand out event refreshments if scheduled; ensure zero trash in labs; upload final attendance logs.
