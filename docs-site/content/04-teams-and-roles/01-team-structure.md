## Inter-Division Collaboration

Each event or software release involves synchronized collaboration:
- **Technical Division** develops registration APIs and verifies digital certificates.
- **Design Division** crafts promotional assets, event banners, and badge designs.
- **Marketing Division** publishes announcements, manages speaker features, and runs social campaigns.
- **Event Operations Division** manages on-campus permissions, lab setups, and attendee check-ins.
- **Administration Division** provides financial approvals, oversight, and post-event analytics.
