# Admin & Leadership Roles

---

## 1. Faculty Advisor
- **Authority:** Highest institutional authority representing Sri Vasavi Engineering College administration.
- **Key Duties:** Approves annual budget outlines, endorses official permissions for venue and lab use, validates academic credits/certificates, and resolves institutional escalations.

---

## 2. Lead Ambassador / President
- **Authority:** Chief student executive of MLSC SVEC.
- **Key Duties:**
  - Official liaison with Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador global management.
  - Steers organizational strategy, annual OKRs, and partnerships.
  - Presides over council meetings and signs off on major releases.

---

## 3. Vice President / Operations Head
- **Authority:** Chief operational coordinator.
- **Key Duties:**
  - Manages cross-team timelines, tracks deliverables, and unblocks Domain Leads.
  - Supervises recruitment logistics and internal team audits.
  - Ensures all procedures adhere to documented SOPs on `docs.mlscsvec.com`.

---

## 4. Portal & System Administrators
- **Authority:** Full administrative control over `mlscsvec.com`, database consoles, and communication bots.
- **Key Duties:**
  - Maintain security rules, manage user role upgrades, and audit logs.
  - Review and publish announcements, events, and ticket tiers.
  - Ensure zero credential leakage and rapid incident response.
