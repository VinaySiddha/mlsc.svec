# Marketing & Outreach Team

The Marketing & Outreach Division amplifies MLSC SVEC's initiatives, connects with student communities, manages public relations, and builds external industry and sponsor partnerships.

---

## 1. Core Functions

- **Social Media Management:** Operating official channels on LinkedIn, Instagram, X (Twitter), and YouTube with scheduled, high-engagement content.
- **Content & Copywriting:** Writing engaging event descriptions, technical articles for `/blog`, newsletter dispatches, and press releases.
- **Campus Outreach & Classroom Campaigns:** Coordinating physical outreach across engineering departments and managing student campus ambassadors.
- **Sponsorship & Speaker Relations:** Reaching out to industry speakers, developer advocates, alumni, and tech companies for sponsorships and hackathon prizes.

---

## 2. Marketing Publishing Checklist

Before any promotional broadcast goes live, verify:
- [ ] Event Title, Date, Time, and Venue match the approved event charter.
- [ ] Direct registration link (`https://mlscsvec.com/events/...`) is verified and functional.
- [ ] Official hashtags (`#MLSC`, `#MLSA`, `#MicrosoftLearn`, `#SVEC`) and collaborator tags are included.
- [ ] Spellings of speaker names, titles, and sponsor logos are verified.
