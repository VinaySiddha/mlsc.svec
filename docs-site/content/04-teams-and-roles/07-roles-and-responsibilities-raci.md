# Roles & Responsibilities (RACI Matrix)

To eliminate ambiguity and overlapping responsibilities, MLSC SVEC uses the **RACI Framework**:
- **R - Responsible:** The role that performs the activity/task.
- **A - Accountable:** The single role with ultimate approval authority and ownership.
- **C - Consulted:** Key stakeholders who provide vital inputs and review.
- **I - Informed:** People who are kept updated on progress and results.
