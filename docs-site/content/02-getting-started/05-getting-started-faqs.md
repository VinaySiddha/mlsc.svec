# Getting Started FAQs

---

### Q: Who is eligible to join MLSC SVEC?
**A:** Any currently enrolled student at Sri Vasavi Engineering College (across all B.Tech branches: CSE, AIML, CST, IT, ECE, EEE, ME, CE, and MBA/MCA programs) is eligible to join as a member and apply for core volunteer positions.

---

### Q: Do I need prior coding experience to join?
**A:** No. General membership requires only a genuine curiosity and willingness to learn. Our Study Hub and beginner bootcamps start from the absolute fundamentals and progressively advance to professional development.

---

### Q: How do I access the Study Hub and track my progress?
**A:** Sign in at [https://mlscsvec.com/study](https://mlscsvec.com/study). As you complete problem sets and tutorial milestones, click the checkmark button next to each problem. Your rank on the live leaderboard will update automatically.

---

### Q: How can I become a Core Volunteer or Team Lead?
**A:** We open official volunteer recruitments at the beginning of each academic semester via [mlscsvec.com/apply](https://mlscsvec.com/apply). Keep an eye on our WhatsApp and Discord channels for announcements.
