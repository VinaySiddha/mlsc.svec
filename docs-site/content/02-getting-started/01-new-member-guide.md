# New Member Guide

Welcome to the **Microsoft Learn Student Club, SVEC** family! This guide outlines everything you need to know to get started, set up your learning trackers, and make the most out of your membership.

---

## Step 1: Create & Verify Your Profile

1. Navigate to [https://mlscsvec.com](https://mlscsvec.com) and click **Sign In** (or **Login**).
2. Authenticate using your Google or college email account.
3. Complete your profile details:
   - Full Name
   - Branch & Academic Year
   - GitHub & LinkedIn profile links
   - Primary Technical Interests (AI/ML, Cloud, Web Dev, DSA)

---

## Step 2: Join Official Communication Channels

To never miss an announcement or event, connect to all official channels:
- **Discord:** Join the [MLSC SVEC Discord](https://discord.gg/mlsc-svec). Head to `#verification` to claim your `@Member` role.
- **WhatsApp Community:** Join the Announcement Group for high-priority updates.
- **Telegram Broadcast:** Subscribe to `@mlscsvec_official` for event reminders and slides.

---

## Step 3: Explore the Study Hub

The [Study Hub](https://mlscsvec.com/study) is your personal technical accelerator:
- **Interactive Sheets:** Track your daily progress across curated DSA problem sets, System Design blueprints, and Web Development modules.
- **Striver's SDE Sheet:** Solve 180+ interview questions with embedded video walkthroughs and portal links.
- **Trophy Leaderboard:** Check off solved problems to accumulate points and climb the global campus leaderboard.

---

## Step 4: Attend Workshops & Earn Verifiable Certificates

1. Browse upcoming workshops and hackathons at [mlscsvec.com/events](https://mlscsvec.com/events).
2. Register for sessions that align with your career goals.
3. Check in during live events using your unique event QR code.
4. Download your tamper-proof, cryptographic digital certificate from `mlscsvec.com/id/[your-id]`.
